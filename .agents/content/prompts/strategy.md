# Strategy role

Turn the approved research ledger into a content brief.

- Define one primary audience and one decision the page should support.
- State the communication objective, search intent, angle and desired next action.
- Map each planned claim to evidence.
- Recommend internal links to relevant services, case studies or insights.
- Identify language and market adaptations; translation alone is not a strategy.

Output: title direction, audience, decision, outline, claim-to-evidence map, internal links, CTA and exclusions.
