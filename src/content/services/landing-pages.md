title: Landing Pages
number: "02"
group: Build
short: Turn campaign attention into one clear, measurable next step.
intro: Give every campaign a focused destination that matches the promise in the ad, removes unnecessary friction and makes enquiry, booking or purchase easier to complete.
painPoints:
  - Paid traffic lands on a generic homepage with too many messages and no obvious next step.
  - The ad promises one thing while the page leads with something else.
  - Forms ask too much, fail on mobile or send enquiries into an unreliable follow-up process.
  - Campaign results are difficult to interpret because the page, tracking and media tests are disconnected.
solutions:
  - title: Match intent to message
    description: The page is built for a specific audience, offer and traffic source so visitors immediately recognise that they are in the right place.
  - title: Build a persuasive sequence
    description: The offer, benefits, proof, objections and call to action appear in the order a buyer needs—not the order internal teams happen to provide them.
  - title: Remove conversion friction
    description: Mobile layout, form fields, loading behaviour and calls to action are simplified around one primary conversion goal.
  - title: Prepare useful experiments
    description: Tracking and test hypotheses are defined before launch so changes are guided by evidence rather than random redesigns.
idealFor:
  - Meta, Google, TikTok or LinkedIn campaigns that need a dedicated destination
  - New offers that should be validated before a full website rebuild
  - Lead-generation campaigns using forms, WhatsApp or booking links
  - Teams that need faster campaign launches and cleaner testing
outcomes:
  - Stronger alignment between ad and page
  - A simpler path to conversion
  - More reliable lead routing and tracking
  - Clearer learning from campaign tests
deliverables:
  - Audience, offer and conversion-goal definition
  - Page strategy, wireframe and copy direction
  - Responsive landing-page design and build
  - Forms, WhatsApp or booking integration
  - Analytics events and campaign tracking plan
  - Quality assurance and launch support
  - Test backlog for future optimisation
process:
  - title: Focus the campaign
    description: Define one primary audience, one offer and the action that signals meaningful intent.
  - title: Shape the argument
    description: Map the promise, proof, objections and information a visitor needs before acting.
  - title: Design and build
    description: Create the responsive page, form experience and reusable components around the agreed conversion path.
  - title: Connect measurement
    description: Add campaign parameters, analytics events and lead-routing checks before traffic is sent to the page.
  - title: Launch and learn
    description: Review behaviour and campaign feedback, then prioritise the next test based on the strongest constraint.
faqs:
  - question: Do I need a full website before building a landing page?
    answer: No. A focused landing page can launch independently, live within your current website or become the first part of a larger website system. The right option depends on trust requirements, campaign duration and how the offer will evolve.
  - question: Can you write the landing-page copy?
    answer: Yes. Copy direction and message hierarchy are part of the work. Full copywriting can be included once the audience, offer, proof and compliance requirements are clear.
  - question: Can the page connect to my CRM or WhatsApp?
    answer: Yes. Forms, booking links, WhatsApp actions, email notifications and CRM routing can be planned as part of the conversion path. The exact integration depends on your current tools and consent requirements.
  - question: Do you guarantee a conversion rate?
    answer: No responsible partner can guarantee a result without traffic quality, offer strength and historical context. I build a stronger conversion environment and a measurement plan so the page can be improved with real evidence.
