## Translate the strategy, then adapt the expression

A multilingual campaign should share the same business objective, offer and measurement logic across markets. But the way it earns attention may change with language, cultural context and channel behaviour.

Direct translation can preserve meaning while losing tone. Build a central message architecture, then let each language find a natural rhythm and the right level of explanation.

- Keep the business promise consistent.
- Adapt examples and proof.
- Review tone with native fluency.
- Measure by market before combining results.

> This article is general marketing guidance, not a guarantee of performance.
