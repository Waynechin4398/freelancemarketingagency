short: Make every advertising decision answer to the full customer journey.
intro: Plan and manage Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn campaigns with the creative, landing experience, tracking and follow-up treated as one acquisition system.
painPoints:
  - Reports show clicks and impressions, but the business still cannot see which campaigns create useful enquiries.
  - Campaigns keep changing audiences and settings without producing clear learning.
  - Creative fatigue is treated as a media-buying problem instead of a message and production problem.
  - Leads arrive, but slow routing or inconsistent follow-up makes advertising performance look worse than it is.
solutions:
  - title: Fix measurement before scale
    description: I map the conversion path, tracking gaps and lead handoffs before using larger budgets to amplify an unclear system.
  - title: Give each channel a role
    description: Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn are selected for the demand, audience and buying journey they can realistically serve—not because every brand needs every platform.
  - title: Build a creative learning loop
    description: Hooks, offers, formats and proof are organised into controlled tests so each campaign produces usable insight for the next one.
  - title: Optimise beyond the ad account
    description: Landing-page friction, lead quality and follow-up feedback are reviewed alongside platform data to find the real constraint.
idealFor:
  - Businesses already spending on ads without reliable lead visibility
  - Teams preparing to launch a new acquisition channel or offer
  - B2B and B2C lead-generation campaigns with a clear sales process
  - Companies that need multilingual campaigns across English, Chinese or Malay
outcomes:
  - Clearer visibility from spend to meaningful action
  - More disciplined testing and budget decisions
  - Stronger alignment between creative, page and offer
  - Better feedback between marketing and lead follow-up
deliverables:
  - Account, funnel and tracking audit
  - Channel and campaign architecture
  - Audience, keyword and offer strategy
  - Creative testing plan and production direction
  - Landing-page and lead-flow recommendations
  - Optimisation cadence and decision log
  - Reporting framework focused on business intent
process:
  - title: Audit the acquisition path
    description: Review account history, offer, creative, landing experience, tracking and what happens after a lead arrives.
  - title: Define the signal
    description: Agree on the actions and lead-quality feedback that should guide budget decisions.
  - title: Build controlled tests
    description: Launch a focused combination of audience, message, creative and destination rather than changing everything at once.
  - title: Read the whole funnel
    description: Compare platform data with page behaviour, lead quality and follow-up outcomes to find the strongest constraint.
  - title: Scale with evidence
    description: Move budget toward combinations that earn it while maintaining a pipeline of new creative and hypotheses.
faqs:
  - question: Which advertising platforms do you manage?
    answer: I work across Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn. The recommendation depends on where demand exists, how your audience researches and whether the campaign goal is immediate response, lead generation or longer-consideration growth.
  - question: How much advertising budget do I need?
    answer: There is no responsible universal minimum. The budget must be large enough to reach the audience and generate useful learning within your sales economics. I will assess the offer, market, conversion path and available creative before recommending a channel and test plan.
  - question: Is creative included?
    answer: Creative strategy, testing angles and production direction are part of the service. The exact volume and production format—static, video, copy variations or collaboration with your internal team—is defined in the scope.
  - question: Can you guarantee ROAS or cost per lead?
    answer: No. Performance depends on the market, offer, budget, creative, page, tracking and sales response. I provide transparent decisions, disciplined testing and full-funnel diagnosis rather than unsupported performance promises.
