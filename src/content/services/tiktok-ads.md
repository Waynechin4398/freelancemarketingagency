  - question: Is TikTok Ads worth it for a service business in Malaysia?

    answer: Spark Ads promote an existing TikTok post from your account or a creator's, keeping its likes and comments. Normal in-feed ads are uploaded only as ads. Spark Ads often feel more native, but need the post owner's authorisation.
