## Scale the journey, not just the media budget

A campaign can only perform as well as the path it creates. If the ad attracts the right person but the landing page changes the promise, the form asks too much, or the follow-up arrives without context, more spend simply sends more people into the same friction.

Before increasing budget, draw the operating path from impression to first human response. Give each stage an owner, a measurable event and a clear definition of what good looks like.

- Align one audience and one promise.
- Measure the handoff, not only the click.
- Route leads with useful context.
- Use reporting to decide what changes next.

> This article is general marketing guidance, not a guarantee of performance. Outcomes depend on the offer, market, execution and measurement conditions.
