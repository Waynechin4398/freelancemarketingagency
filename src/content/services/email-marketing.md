title: Email Marketing
number: "05"
group: Engage
short: Keep valuable conversations moving after the first click.
intro: Build timely email journeys for new enquiries, consideration, conversion and retention—so follow-up reflects what each person asked for and what should happen next.
painPoints:
  - New enquiries receive a generic acknowledgement and then depend entirely on manual follow-up.
  - Every contact receives the same message regardless of source, interest or stage.
  - Newsletters are sent irregularly without a clear role in the customer journey.
  - Open and click reports exist, but the team cannot connect email activity to useful business actions.
solutions:
  - title: Map the lifecycle first
    description: Email is planned around real moments—new enquiry, consideration, onboarding, re-engagement or retention—rather than an isolated list of campaigns.
  - title: Match message to intent
    description: Segments, triggers and content reflect what the person requested, where they came from and what decision they are trying to make.
  - title: Balance automation and people
    description: Routine guidance and reminders can be automated while important replies and high-intent conversations remain visible to the right person.
  - title: Measure the next action
    description: Reporting focuses on movement through the journey, not open rate alone, with links to the website, CRM or sales process where appropriate.
idealFor:
  - Lead-generation businesses that need faster, more consistent follow-up
  - Companies with a contact list but no lifecycle or segmentation plan
  - Teams launching an onboarding, nurture or re-engagement sequence
  - Brands that need multilingual email journeys across markets
outcomes:
  - Faster and more relevant follow-up
  - More consistent lead nurturing
  - Clearer lifecycle stages and ownership
  - Better visibility into content that moves people forward
deliverables:
  - Lifecycle and customer-journey map
  - Segmentation and trigger strategy
  - Sequence structure and email copy
  - Responsive email layout direction
  - Forms, consent and list-entry review
  - Automation and handoff specification
  - Measurement dashboard and optimisation plan
process:
  - title: Map intent and stages
    description: Define why contacts enter the list, what they need at each stage and which actions indicate progress.
  - title: Design the sequence
    description: Choose triggers, timing, branches and human handoffs before writing individual emails.
  - title: Write and build
    description: Develop useful messages, calls to action and responsive layouts that remain consistent with the wider brand journey.
  - title: Connect and test
    description: Configure the agreed platform, data fields and automations, then test links, timing, consent and fallback paths.
  - title: Learn from behaviour
    description: Review engagement and downstream actions to improve subject lines, content, timing and segmentation.
faqs:
  - question: Can you work with my current email platform?
    answer: Usually, yes. I first review the platform, list quality, forms, fields and integrations. If the current tool cannot support the required lifecycle or reporting, I will explain the limitation before recommending a change.
  - question: Do you write the email copy?
    answer: Yes. Sequence strategy and copy can be included together so each message has a clear purpose, context and next step. Brand inputs, offer details and any regulated claims must be supplied or approved by your team.
  - question: Can you clean or use an existing contact list?
    answer: I can help review segmentation and list health, but contacts should only be messaged where the business has an appropriate permission and lawful basis. Purchased or unclear lists are not a sound foundation for the programme.
  - question: How do we measure whether email is working?
    answer: Opens and clicks are useful diagnostic signals, not the whole result. Measurement should also reflect replies, bookings, qualified actions, onboarding progress or retention behaviour appropriate to the sequence.
