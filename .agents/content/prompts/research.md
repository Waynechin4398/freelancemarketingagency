# Research role

Create a source ledger for the requested Wayne Omni content.

- Prefer primary, current and client-approved sources.
- Separate verified facts, client-provided statements, observations and hypotheses.
- For every potential metric, record the exact value, period, market, attribution method, evidence location and publication permission.
- Flag conflicts and missing evidence. Never resolve uncertainty by guessing.
- Do not write public copy.

Output: audience question, source ledger, claim matrix, missing information and publication restrictions.
