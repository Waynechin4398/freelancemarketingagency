## Most conversion problems are quiet

The page can look polished and still make the decision unnecessarily hard. Common friction appears when the headline is broader than the ad, proof arrives after the visitor has already hesitated, or several equal calls to action compete for attention.

A stronger page does not always need more content. It needs the right information in the order the visitor needs it.

1. The promise changes after the click.
2. The next action is visually unclear.
3. Proof lacks context.
4. The form asks before trust is built.
5. Mobile comparison is harder than desktop.
